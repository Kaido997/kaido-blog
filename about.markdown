I'm Alan (Kaido) — backend developer with 7+ years on the CNC shop floor.

I build software for the manufacturing industry I know from the inside. Currently a CNC Programmer at Millar Gear while working on tools like [food-weight-calculator](https://foodweight.online), NC++ (a C++20 CNC programming language), and Visual CNC (a G-code toolpath visualizer).

Before writing production software I spent six years programming CNC machines at MVV srl in Italy — Fanuc 3-axis and Mazak Integrex 5-axis. That background shapes how I build: manufacturing teaches you that precision isn't optional and edge cases aren't hypothetical.

On this blog I write about things I'm building and the intersection of manufacturing and software — things only someone who's been on the shop floor can write.

Previously: backend at Cardinal (Adyen payments, GraphQL, Web3); backend at Hyper Foundry (AI consultation platform). Self-taught developer, Professional Certification at Start2Impact.

Based in Toronto, Canada. Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/Kaido997) and [X](https://x.com/pirro_alan).
